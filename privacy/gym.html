Health and Fitness Data

Gym is a workout tracking app, so the information you enter is health and fitness data. We treat it as the most sensitive category of data the App handles, and this section explains exactly what we access, collect and do with it.

What health and fitness data we collect

All of the following is created by you, inside the App, when you use it:

  • Workout and exercise data — the exercises you perform, sets, repetitions, weight lifted, rest times, workout duration, workout dates and times, routines and training plans you build, and notes you attach to a workout.
  • Body measurement data — body weight, height, and any other body measurements you choose to record (for example body-fat percentage or circumference measurements), together with values derived from them such as BMI.
  • Fitness profile data — optional details such as age or date of birth, sex, and training goals, where you choose to enter them so the App can present your progress correctly.
  • Progress and history data — your training history, personal records, streaks, volume and progress charts calculated from the entries above.

We do not collect health data in the background. The App does not read from device sensors on its own, and it does not silently monitor your activity. Health and fitness data enters the App only when you type it in or explicitly save it.

How we access and store it

Health and fitness data you enter is stored on your device in the App's private storage. We access it in order to display it back to you, to compute your history, progress and personal records, and to power the App's tracking features.

If your device is configured to back up app data (for example Android Auto Backup to your own Google Drive account), a copy of this data may be included in that backup. That backup is controlled by you and by Google under your Google account settings, and we cannot read it.

How we use it

We use your health and fitness data solely to:

  • record and display your workouts and body measurements;
  • calculate progress, totals, personal records and charts for you;
  • let you plan, repeat and edit routines; and
  • provide the core functionality of the App you asked for.

What we never do with it

  • We do not sell your health or fitness data.
  • We do not use it for advertising, ad targeting, or to build marketing profiles, and we do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers.
  • We do not share it with other users of the App or make it public.
  • We do not transfer it to third parties, except in the narrow legal circumstances described under Disclosure of your information.

Deleting your health and fitness data

You can delete individual workouts, measurements and routines from within the App at any time. Uninstalling the App removes the health and fitness data held in the App's private storage on that device. You may also email us at nothingdeveloped@gmail.com with a deletion request, and we will respond within 30 days.

Other Data We Collect

Data you provide

Besides the health and fitness data described above, you may provide an app nickname or profile name and your unit preferences (kg/lb, cm/in). If you contact us for support, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message, and we use it only to answer you.

Usage and diagnostic data

When you use the App we may collect limited technical information automatically, such as device model, operating system version, app version, general app usage events, and crash reports containing diagnostic stack traces. This is used only to keep the App working, diagnose crashes and improve stability. Crash and diagnostic reports do not include your workout entries or body measurements.

Service Providers

We may use a small number of third-party service providers to operate the App — for example an app-distribution and crash-reporting platform. Retention

Health and fitness data you enter stays on your device until you delete it or uninstall the App. Diagnostic and crash data is retained only as long as needed to investigate and fix the underlying problem, and for no longer than is required by applicable law.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including relying on the operating system's app-sandboxing so that other apps cannot read the App's private storage. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Disclosure of Your Information

We may disclose your information only in these situations:

  • With your consent — where you explicitly ask us to.
  • Legal requirements — where we are required to do so by law, or in response to a valid request by a public authority such as a court or government agency.
  • Protection of rights and safety — where disclosure is reasonably necessary to investigate possible wrongdoing in connection with the App, protect the personal safety of users or the public, or defend against legal liability.
  • Business transfer — if the App is transferred to another developer, your information may be transferred as part of it. We will post notice in this policy before your information becomes subject to a different privacy policy, and health and fitness data will remain subject to protections at least as strong as those described here.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to withdraw consent. Because health and fitness data is stored on your device, you can exercise most of these rights directly in the App. For anything else, contact us at nothingdeveloped@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information — including health or fitness data — from anyone under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with such information, contact us at nothingdeveloped@gmail.com and we will delete it. Where consent from a parent is required in your country, we will obtain that consent before collecting or using the information.
